# Scanners Spec

## Purpose

Scanners turn verified intelligence into structured review candidates.

They do not trade. They surface items for review.

## Authority

This spec must align with [docs/TRADER_AI_CONSTITUTION.md](./TRADER_AI_CONSTITUTION.md).

## Scanner Modules

### Long-Term Investment Scanner

Looks for:

- ETFs
- Blue chips
- Dividend stocks

Signals:

- Durable trend
- Quality of evidence
- Portfolio fit
- Stable thesis

### Swing Trade Scanner

Looks for:

- Breakouts
- Earnings momentum
- Sector rotation

Signals:

- Price expansion
- Volume confirmation
- Catalyst timing
- Manageable horizon

### Penny Share Scanner

Looks for:

- AIM names
- Small caps
- Mining explorers
- Resource companies

Signals:

- Liquidity
- Volatility
- Catalyst credibility
- Source quality

### Mining Resource Scanner

Looks for:

- Drill results
- Resource updates
- Commodity sensitivity
- Financing risk

Signals:

- Commodity tailwind
- New assay data
- Corporate funding events

### Special Situations Scanner

Looks for:

- Takeovers
- Regulatory approvals
- Rights issues
- Spin-offs

Signals:

- Event timing
- Structural change
- Price dislocation

### ETF And Sector Rotation Scanner

Looks for:

- Macro regime changes
- Relative strength
- Sector leadership

Signals:

- Rotation evidence
- Allocation relevance
- Macro alignment

## Morning Scan

The morning scan should surface:

- Market health
- Overnight developments
- New opportunities
- Earnings calendar items
- Major filings
- High-priority reviews

## Evening Scan

The evening scan should surface:

- Market summary
- Portfolio changes
- New intelligence
- Watchlist changes
- Opportunities for tomorrow

## Alert Thresholds

Alert thresholds should be conservative.

Suggested triggers:

- New verified catalyst
- Significant confidence change
- Material risk escalation
- Event date approaching
- Watchlist item crossing a review threshold
- Portfolio item moving into higher-risk status

Rules:

- Avoid alert spam.
- Prefer fewer, more useful alerts.
- Do not alert on low-confidence noise.
